ASCII Studio Workflow

            
How the client-side engine works:

            
    
                
  • AI Prompts: This app downloads a real Hugging Face embedding model (Transformers.js) into your browser on first run. It semantically maps your prompt to an abstract procedural generation, completely offline.
    • 
                
  • Source Images: Uploaded images bypass the AI and are perfectly pixel-mapped to ASCII blocks based on contrast and luminance.
    • 
                
  • Aspect Ratios: The generator automatically scales grid rows to compensate for the vertical height of monospace fonts, keeping your output undistorted.
